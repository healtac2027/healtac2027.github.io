The programme for HealTAC 2027 will be published on this page once the dates, venue and
accepted contributions have been confirmed.

As in previous years, the conference is expected to include:

- keynote talks
- oral presentations and flash/lightning talks
- a poster and software demo session
- a PhD and early career researcher forum
- panel discussions
- pre- or post-conference workshops and tutorials

For the shape of a typical HealTAC programme, see the
[previous conferences](/previous-conferences/) in the series.
