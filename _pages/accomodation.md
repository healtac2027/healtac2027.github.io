## Travelling to Sheffield

### By rail

Sheffield station is in the city centre, about a 20-minute walk or a short tram ride from the
University's main campus. Approximate direct journey times:

| From | Approximate time |
|---|---|
| London St Pancras International | about 2 hours 10 minutes |
| Manchester Piccadilly | about 1 hour |
| Birmingham New Street | about 1 hour 20 minutes |
| Leeds | about 1 hour |
| Edinburgh / Newcastle | direct CrossCountry services |

Times vary by service and operator — check [National Rail](https://www.nationalrail.co.uk/) for
current timetables. Booking in advance is usually considerably cheaper than buying on the day.

### By air

**Manchester Airport** is the most convenient international gateway, with a direct train to
Sheffield taking roughly an hour and a half. **Birmingham**, **Leeds Bradford**, **East
Midlands** and the **London** airports are all also usable, connecting onward by rail.

### By road

Sheffield is close to the M1 (junctions 33–36) and linked to Manchester by the M67/A57 and the
Snake Pass. Note that city-centre parking is limited and charged; if you are driving, check
parking with your hotel in advance.

## Getting around

The city centre is easily walkable. The **Sheffield Supertram** and local bus network connect
the station, the city centre and the University campus. Taxis and the usual ride-hailing apps
operate throughout the city.

## Accommodation

Suggested hotels near the venue, along with any reserved delegate rates or booking codes, will be
published on this page once the venue and dates are confirmed. Sheffield has a good range of
hotels and university accommodation within walking distance of both the station and the campus.

## Visas

Delegates who need a visa to enter the UK should check the
[UK government guidance](https://www.gov.uk/check-uk-visa) well in advance. Once registration
opens, the conference organisers will be able to provide invitation letters for visa
applications.
