HealTAC 2027 will be hosted by the [University of Sheffield](https://www.sheffield.ac.uk/) in
**Sheffield, United Kingdom**. The specific venue will be published on this page, together with
the address, maps, accessibility information and directions from the railway station.

## About Sheffield

Sheffield is a city of around 550,000 people in South Yorkshire, in the north of England. It
grew up around steel and cutlery — "Sheffield steel" — and is now a city of two large
universities and a substantial healthcare and research sector.

Roughly a third of the city lies inside the **Peak District National Park**, and it is among the
greenest cities in the UK, so the conference is within easy reach of open moorland and walking
country. The city centre is compact and walkable, and the University's main campus sits just to
the west of it.

## Healthcare text analytics in Sheffield

Sheffield has a long history in the field. The University's
[Natural Language Processing group](https://www.sheffield.ac.uk/dcs/research/groups/natural-language-processing)
in the School of Computer Science, and the [GATE](https://gate.ac.uk/) team — whose open-source
text engineering framework is widely used for clinical and biomedical text — have worked on
healthcare language processing for many years.
