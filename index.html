HealTAC 2027: 10th Healthcare Text Analytics Conference

Sheffield, United Kingdom

Welcome to the 10th Healthcare Text Analytics Conference, which will be held in Sheffield, hosted by the University of Sheffield. The conference dates are to be confirmed. As in previous years, HealTAC 2027 will bring the academic, clinical, industrial and patient communities together to discuss the current state of the art in processing healthcare free text and share experience, results and challenges.

We will invite contributions that address any aspects of processing healthcare narrative and speech, including methodological advances, ethical issues, design and deployment challenges and integration with other healthcare data on large-scale. This year's theme is to be confirmed. The call for contributions will be available here.

The draft programme will be made available here.

The hashtag for the event: #HEALTAC2027

Why Sheffield

Sheffield sits between the Pennines and the Peak District, with roughly a third of the city inside the national park — it is one of the greenest cities in the UK. It is also a long-standing centre for healthcare text analytics: the University of Sheffield's Natural Language Processing group and the GATE team have worked on clinical and biomedical language processing for many years. The city is compact and walkable, and well connected by rail to London, Manchester, Birmingham and Leeds. See the Venue and Accommodation and Travel pages for details.
